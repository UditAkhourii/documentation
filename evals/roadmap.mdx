The live backlog is the [issue tracker](https://github.com/UditAkhourii/adhd/issues). Headline items:

- **Recursive deepen** — multi-level Tree-of-Thought expansion, not just one level
- **Pluggable scorers** — user-defined weights and custom trap detectors
- **Frame packs** — security, ML, frontend, distsys, product
- **Memory across runs** — learn which frames win for which problem shapes ([issue #10](https://github.com/UditAkhourii/adhd/issues/10))
- **Streaming output** during divergence
- **Cross-LLM support** — frames don't depend on Claude
- **Cross-model judging** for the eval suite ([issue #6](https://github.com/UditAkhourii/adhd/issues/6))
- **Hyperfocus / flow-state companion skill** ([issue #11](https://github.com/UditAkhourii/adhd/issues/11))

## Open research threads

- [Issue #16](https://github.com/UditAkhourii/adhd/issues/16) — human-in-the-loop applicability (CHI 2025 counter-evidence)

- [Issue #18](https://github.com/UditAkhourii/adhd/issues/18) — bridging the K=5 vs K=100 scale gap in the diversity literature
- [Issue #13](https://github.com/UditAkhourii/adhd/issues/13) — a controlled `parallel-N` vs `single-prompt-list-N` comparison
