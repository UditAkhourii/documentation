People with ADHD often benefit from external motivation and immediate feedback. The ADHD Focus rewards system turns productivity into a game by giving you dopamine-boosting points, badges, and levels for the work you already do. Every completed task, finished focus session, and checked-in habit earns you progress toward a higher Focus Score.

## How you earn points

Points are awarded automatically when you complete actions in the app. The more consistent you are, the faster your Focus Score grows.

| Action | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Complete a task | 10 pts |
| Finish a focus session | 20 pts |
| Check in a habit | 5 pts |
| Hit your daily goal | 50 pts |
| Maintain a 7-day habit streak | 25 pts |
| Complete a weekly review | 30 pts |

Daily goals are calculated based on your personal setup and can include a mix of tasks, sessions, and habits.

## Levels and badges

Your **Focus Score** is a single number that reflects your overall productivity and consistency. As your score increases, you level up through tiers such as Starter, Focused, Driven, and Flow State. Each level unlocks new visual themes and profile flair.

**Badges** reward specific achievements. Examples include:

- **First Focus** — Complete your first focus session
- **Streak Starter** — Check in a habit for 7 days
- **Task Master** — Complete 50 tasks
- **Deep Diver** — Finish a 60-minute focus session
- **Week Warrior** — Hit your daily goal 7 days in a row

Badges appear on your profile and can be shared if you choose.

<Tip>
  Set a personal reward for hitting a weekly goal, such as watching a favorite show, buying a small treat, or taking a guilt-free nap. Pairing app points with real-world rewards strengthens the motivation loop.
</Tip>
