Standard to-do apps often fail ADHD users because they demand upfront organization, hide tasks behind menus, and punish missed deadlines. ADHD Focus flips that model. You capture tasks quickly, let the app help you prioritize, and break intimidating projects into tiny, doable steps so starting feels possible.

## Core features

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Brain Dump" icon="cloud-arrow-down">
    Capture every task, idea, and worry in one place without sorting first. Just type and save. Organize later when your brain is ready.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Priority Sort" icon="arrow-up-wide-short">
    Let the app's AI-assisted urgency ranking surface what matters most today. You stay in control, but you don't have to decide everything from scratch.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Micro-tasks" icon="scissors">
    Break any task into 2-minute chunks. "Write essay" becomes "Open document," "Write intro sentence," and "List three bullet points."
  </Card>

  <Card title="Task Timer" icon="clock">
    Attach a Focus Session directly to a task. Tap the timer icon on any task to start a session pre-filled with the right length and goal.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Create and complete a task

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Brain Dump">
    Tap **Brain Dump** from the home screen or task list. Type whatever is on your mind, then tap **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and sort">
    Go to your task list and tap **Sort**. ADHD Focus suggests a priority order based on deadlines and your past patterns. Drag tasks to adjust the order if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Break it down">
    Tap any task and select **Make Micro-tasks**. The app suggests small steps. Edit or add your own, then tap **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start with one micro-task">
    Pick the first micro-task and tap the **Timer** icon to launch a Focus Session. Complete the step, then mark it done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark the parent task complete">
    When all micro-tasks are finished, the parent task auto-completes. Or tap **Done** manually if you finished it early.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  When your mind is racing, use Brain Dump first and sort later. Capturing everything fast prevents ideas from evaporating, and sorting is easier once they're out of your head.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Try to keep your active task list to 3-5 items at a time. A long list creates decision paralysis. Move everything else to Brain Dump or a "Later" list so your today-view stays manageable.
</Warning>
