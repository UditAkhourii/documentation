Building habits can feel especially difficult with ADHD. Traditional trackers punish missed days with broken streaks, which often leads to shame and giving up entirely. ADHD Focus takes a gentler approach. Flexible streaks, visual progress rings, and kind reminders help you build momentum without the pressure of perfection.

## Creating a habit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Habits tab">
    Open the app and tap **Habits** in the bottom navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap + New Habit">
    Tap the **\+** button in the top-right corner to start creating a new habit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and choose a frequency">
    Give your habit a clear, actionable name like "Drink a glass of water" or "Take meds." Then choose how often you want to do it: **Daily**, **Weekdays only**, or a **Custom** schedule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a reminder time">
    Pick a time that fits your routine. The reminder will be gentle and will not repeat if you dismiss it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a color and icon">
    Choose a color and icon that make the habit feel fun and personal. Visual cues make habits easier to remember.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Flexible streaks

ADHD Focus uses a grace-day system so that missing one day does not immediately break your streak. If you miss a single day, your streak stays intact. If you miss two days in a row, the streak pauses and you start again from one. This removes the all-or-nothing pressure that causes many people with ADHD to abandon habit tracking after a single slip.

Your progress ring shows how close you are to your weekly target, not just a single chain of consecutive days. This shifts the focus from perfection to consistency over time.

<Tip>
  Link your new habit to something you already do. This technique, called habit stacking, makes it easier to remember. For example, "After I pour my morning coffee, I will take my vitamins."
</Tip>

<Note>
  Every habit check-in earns reward points. These points add to your Focus Score and can help you unlock badges and level up over time.
</Note>
