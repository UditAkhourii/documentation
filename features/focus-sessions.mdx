Focus Sessions are ADHD Focus's core tool for getting things done without burnout. Each session is a timed block of work followed by a short break, designed to match how ADHD brains actually function: in bursts, with clear endpoints, and without the pressure of an open-ended to-do list.

## Key concepts

- **Session length**: Choose anywhere from 10 to 90 minutes. Shorter sessions are great for starting; longer ones work once you're in flow.
- **Break reminders**: Optional prompts to pause and reset between blocks. You decide how often they appear.
- **Ambient sound modes**: Rain, White Noise, and Cafe sounds help mask distractions and create a consistent mental cue that it's time to work.
- **Distraction Blocker**: An optional mode that gently limits access to other apps during a session, so you stay in the loop.

## Start a Focus Session

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap the Focus button">
    Open the app and tap the large **Focus** button on your home screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose session length and break frequency">
    Use the slider to set how long you want to focus. Toggle **Break reminders** on or off, and pick how often you'd like to be reminded to pause.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick an ambient sound (optional)">
    Tap the sound icon and choose **Rain**, **White Noise**, or **Cafe**. Tap again to turn sound off if you prefer silence.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Distraction Blocker (optional)">
    Toggle on **Distraction Blocker** if you want extra help staying on task. You can customize what it blocks in your preferences.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hit Start">
    Tap **Start Session**. The timer begins, and your chosen sound starts playing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you're new to timed focus, start with 20-minute sessions. They're long enough to make progress and short enough that starting doesn't feel overwhelming.
</Tip>

## During a session

While a session is running, you have a few options:

- **Pause**: Tap the pause button if you need to step away. The timer stops and resumes when you return.
- **Add a quick note**: Tap the note icon to jot down a distracting thought without leaving the app. The note is saved to your Brain Dump for later.
- **End early**: Tap **End Session** if you need to stop. There's no penalty, and partial sessions still earn partial reward points.

<Note>
  Every completed minute in a Focus Session earns reward points. Full sessions earn bonus points, which you can spend in the Rewards store. See [Rewards & Motivation](/features/rewards) for details.
</Note>
