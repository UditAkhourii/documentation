Welcome to ADHD Focus, a productivity app built from the ground up for brains that work differently. Whether you struggle with starting tasks, staying on track, or remembering what you meant to do five minutes ago, ADHD Focus meets you where you are. The app turns scattered energy into structured momentum through focus sessions, simple task tools, habit reminders, and rewards that actually feel rewarding.

## Start in three simple steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up with email, Google, or Apple in under a minute. No credit card required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your first focus session">
    Answer a short onboarding quiz to set your ideal focus duration and break style, then launch a session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete a task and earn your first reward">
    Add one task, finish it during a focus session, and collect your first reward to celebrate the win.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Every feature in ADHD Focus is designed with ADHD-friendly defaults: short timers, clear visuals, gentle reminders, and no guilt if you skip a day. You can always adjust settings to match what works for you.
</Tip>
