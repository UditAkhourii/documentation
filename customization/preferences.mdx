ADHD Focus is built to adapt to you, not the other way around. Your Preferences let you set defaults so the app matches your attention style, energy patterns, and workflow from the moment you open it. A few minutes of setup here can remove daily friction and help you build a routine that actually sticks.

## Focus Settings

These defaults shape every focus session you start. Set them once and they apply automatically, though you can still override them for individual sessions.

| Setting | What it does | Suggested starting point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Default session length** | Sets the timer when you start a focus session. | 15–25 minutes if you are new to structured focus; 45 minutes if you already know your sustained attention window. |
| **Default break length** | Sets the rest timer that follows each session. | 5 minutes for short sessions; 10–15 minutes for longer ones. |
| **Auto-start breaks** | When enabled, the break timer begins automatically when a focus session ends. | Turn this on if you tend to skip breaks and crash later. |

## Task Settings

Your task view changes how you interact with your to-do list. Pick the layout that feels least overwhelming for your brain.

| Setting | Options | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Default task view** | List, Board, or Priority | List for linear thinkers; Board for visual planners; Priority for urgent-first workflows. |
| **Show micro-tasks by default** | On or Off | On if large tasks feel paralyzing and you prefer tiny, actionable steps. |

When micro-tasks are enabled, every task you create automatically includes a sub-step field so you can break it down immediately.

## ADHD Profile

Your ADHD Profile helps ADHD Focus tailor suggestions and nudges to your specific patterns. This is not a diagnosis tool; it is a self-assessment you can update anytime.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Attention style** | Choose from _Sustained_ (long blocks), _Variable_ (shorter, flexible sessions), or _Hyperfocus_ (deep dives with hard stop reminders). |
| **Peak hours** | Set the time windows when you usually feel most alert. ADHD Focus will suggest high-priority tasks during these windows and lighter tasks when your energy dips. |

<Tip>
  If you are unsure what session length works for you, start with 15 minutes and adjust by 5-minute increments each week. There is no wrong answer: your optimal length might change based on sleep, stress, or medication timing.
</Tip>
