Your visual environment shapes your ability to focus, especially with ADHD. Cluttered or harsh interfaces can drain cognitive energy before you even begin a task. ADHD Focus lets you control colors, contrast, and font size so the app feels calm, clear, and easy to navigate.

## Available themes

Choose the theme that feels most comfortable for your eyes and attention style.

| Theme | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| **Light** | Bright, clean look for daytime use and well-lit spaces. |
| **Dark** | Reduced eye strain in low-light settings and a calmer visual tone. |
| **High Contrast** | Maximum separation between elements. Ideal if busy interfaces feel distracting or if you experience visual overwhelm from subtle color differences. |
| **Auto** | Follows your system setting, switching between Light and Dark automatically. |

## Accent colors

Pick an accent color to highlight buttons, progress bars, and rewards. The six preset options are designed to be vibrant enough to draw attention without feeling overstimulating.

| Color | Mood |
| --- | --- |
| Calm Blue | Steady and neutral |
| Focus Green | Fresh and motivating |
| Energy Orange | Warm and active |
| Bold Purple | Creative and distinct |
| Sunny Yellow | Cheerful and uplifting |
| Soft Pink | Gentle and friendly |

## Font size

Adjust text size independently of your device settings.

| Option | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Standard** | Default sizing for most users. |
| **Large** | Bigger text for easier scanning and reduced eye strain. |
| **Extra Large** | Maximum readability, helpful if you prefer fewer items on screen at once. |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Tap your profile icon, then choose **Settings** from the menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Appearance">
    Tap **Appearance** to open the theme and color options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your theme">
    Tap **Theme** and select Light, Dark, High Contrast, or Auto.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick an accent color">
    Tap **Accent Color** and choose the shade that feels right for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust font size">
    Tap **Font Size** and select Standard, Large, or Extra Large. The change applies immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you find yourself distracted by subtle gradients, soft edges, or low-contrast buttons, try **High Contrast** mode. It sharpens boundaries across the entire interface and can make navigation feel faster and less mentally taxing.
</Tip>
