ADHD Focus offers a free plan that covers the essentials, plus a Pro plan that unlocks unlimited focus sessions, habits, and advanced tools. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time with no hidden fees.

## Plans at a glance

| Feature | Free | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Focus sessions | Up to 25 minutes | Unlimited duration |
| Habits | 3 active habits | Unlimited habits |
| Rewards | Basic rewards | Full rewards library \+ custom rewards |
| Distraction blocker | Not included | Included |
| Calendar sync | Not included | Included |
| Support | Community support | Priority support |

## Upgrade to Pro

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Tap your profile icon, then tap **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Subscription">
    Tap **Subscription & Plans**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Pro">
    Tap **Upgrade to Pro** and choose monthly or annual billing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm payment">
    Enter your payment details and confirm. Your Pro features activate immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Every new ADHD Focus account gets a free 14-day Pro trial. You will not be charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel anytime during the trial with no charge.
</Note>

## Manage your subscription

You can update your payment method, switch between monthly and annual billing, pause your plan, or cancel entirely from the same **Subscription & Plans** screen in Settings.

If you cancel Pro, you keep Pro access until the end of your current billing period, then your account automatically switches to the Free plan. Your habits, tasks, and history stay saved; only Pro features are limited.

## Billing questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I update my payment method?">
    Go to **Settings** \> **Subscription & Plans** \> **Payment Method**. Enter your new card or payment details and tap **Save**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I pause my subscription instead of canceling?">
    Yes. On the **Subscription & Plans** screen, tap **Pause Plan**. Your Pro access pauses at the end of the current billing period and resumes automatically when you choose. You keep all your data.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will I get a refund if I cancel early?">
    ADHD Focus does not offer partial refunds for unused time. You retain Pro access through the end of your billing period, then the plan downgrades to Free.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
