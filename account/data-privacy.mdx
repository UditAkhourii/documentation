ADHD Focus is built to support you, not to exploit your data. We collect only what is needed to run the app, store it securely, and never sell it to third parties. You are always in control of your information.

## What data we collect

- **Usage data**: App interactions like session starts, timer completions, and feature usage. This helps us improve the app and fix bugs.
- **Habit and task data**: The habits, tasks, focus sessions, streaks, and rewards you create. This is the core of your account.
- **Optional calendar data**: If you connect a calendar for sync, we access only the data needed to display and schedule events within ADHD Focus.
- **Account data**: Your email address, profile name, and subscription status.

We do not collect diagnostic information, browsing history, or data from other apps on your device.

## Data storage and security

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We use industry-standard cloud infrastructure with regular security audits. Your data is never sold, rented, or shared with advertisers. We only share data with service providers who help us run the app (such as cloud hosting and payment processing), and only under strict confidentiality agreements.

## Export your data

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Tap your profile icon, then tap **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Data & Privacy">
    Tap **Data & Privacy**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request export">
    Tap **Export My Data**, then choose your preferred format (JSON or CSV). The export is emailed to you within 24 hours.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Delete your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Tap your profile icon, then tap **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Data & Privacy">
    Tap **Data & Privacy**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete account">
    Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete Account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Enter your password to confirm. Your account and all data will be permanently removed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your habits, tasks, streaks, rewards, and history will be erased immediately. If you think you might return later, consider pausing your subscription instead.
</Warning>
