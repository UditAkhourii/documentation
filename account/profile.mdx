Your profile is the control center for how ADHD Focus recognizes you and adapts to your needs. Keeping it up to date ensures the app continues to suggest session lengths, break styles, and task sizes that match where you are right now. You can change your display name, avatar, ADHD preferences, and linked accounts whenever you like.

## Update your profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings > Profile">
    Tap your avatar in the top corner of the home screen, then choose **Settings \> Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit display name and avatar">
    Tap your current name to edit it. To change your avatar, tap the profile picture and choose a new illustration or upload a photo. Your display name appears on leaderboards, shared sessions, and progress reports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update ADHD preferences">
    In the **ADHD Preferences** section, adjust:

    - **Focus style**: Short bursts, deep dives, or mixed.
    - **Task chunk preference**: Small, medium, or large task slices.
    - **Peak focus times**: Morning, afternoon, evening, or late night. These settings shape the app's default suggestions across focus sessions, task breakdowns, and daily planning.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage connected accounts">
    Scroll to **Connected Accounts** to view or remove linked services such as Google, Apple, or your calendar. Tap **Disconnect** next to any service you no longer want attached. If you used a social login, keep at least one active method so you do not lose access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Changing your ADHD preferences re-tunes the app's suggestions starting with your next session. Past streaks and completed tasks stay exactly as they are.
</Note>
