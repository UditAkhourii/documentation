This guide walks you through everything you need to start using ADHD Focus right now. In about five minutes, you will create your account, personalize the app to match how your brain works, add your first task, and complete your first focus session.

<Note>
  The onboarding quiz takes about two minutes. It helps the app suggest focus lengths, break styles, and reminder timings that fit you, so it is worth completing before you dive in.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download or open the web app">
    Install ADHD Focus from the App Store or Google Play, or open the web app in your browser. Pick the option that feels easiest right now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Tap **Sign Up** and register with your email address, or use **Sign in with Google** or **Sign in with Apple** for a faster setup. You will receive a confirmation email if you choose email registration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the onboarding quiz">
    Answer a few quick questions about your typical focus length, preferred break style, and how you like to view tasks. The app uses these answers to set your default focus session length, reminder frequency, and task display format.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your first task">
    Tap the **Tasks** tab, then tap the **\+** button. Enter a task name, set a due date if you want one, and choose a priority level. Keep the description short and specific so the task feels doable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start your first Focus Session">
    Go to the **Focus** tab, select the task you just added, and tap **Start Session**. The timer will run for the length you chose during onboarding, followed by a break. When the session ends, mark your task complete and collect your first reward.
  </Step>
</Steps>

That is it. You are now ready to use focus sessions, manage tasks, and build habits with a system built for the way your mind works.
