A thoughtful setup makes ADHD Focus feel like it was built just for you. When you share a little about how your attention works, the app can suggest better session lengths, chunk tasks into manageable pieces, and nudge you at the right times. This guide walks you through verifying your account, shaping your ADHD profile, and connecting the tools you already use.

## Complete your setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    After you sign up, ADHD Focus sends a verification link to your email. Open the message and tap **Verify Email** to activate your account. If you do not see the email within a few minutes, check your spam or promotions folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your ADHD profile">
    Open **Settings \> Profile \> ADHD Preferences** and answer a few quick questions:

    - **Attention style**: Do you focus best in short bursts, longer deep dives, or a mix of both?
    - **Peak focus times**: Which parts of the day feel easiest to concentrate?
    - **Preferred task chunk size**: How small should a single to-do feel before it stops overwhelming you? Your answers tune the app's default session lengths, break reminders, and task suggestions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your calendar (optional)">
    Link **Google Calendar** or **Outlook** under **Settings \> Integrations \> Calendar**. Once connected, ADHD Focus can suggest focus blocks around existing events and surface daily task reminders at calmer moments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your daily focus goal">
    Go to **Settings \> Goals** and choose a realistic daily target in minutes. This goal powers your streaks, progress charts, and reward unlocks. You can change it anytime as your routine evolves.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Start with a smaller daily goal than you think you need. A 20-minute target you hit five days in a row builds more momentum than a 90-minute target you miss twice. You can always raise it once the habit sticks.
</Tip>

## Signing in

You can access ADHD Focus using the method that is easiest for you.

| Method | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Email and password** | Enter the email and password you chose during signup. Use **Forgot password** on the login screen if you need a reset link. |
| **Google** | Tap **Continue with Google** and select the account you want to use. No separate password needed. |
| **Apple** | Tap **Continue with Apple** on iOS or the web. You can choose to share or hide your email address. |

If you signed up with Google or Apple and later want to add a password, go to **Settings \> Security \> Set Password**.
