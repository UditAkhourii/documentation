These are the questions we hear most often from new and returning users. Each answer is designed to get you back to focusing quickly.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is ADHD Focus only for people with ADHD?">
    No. While the app is designed with ADHD-friendly features like short focus sessions, visual rewards, and flexible habit tracking, anyone who struggles with focus, procrastination, or consistency can benefit. Many users without a diagnosis find the structure helpful.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How is this different from a regular to-do app?">
    ADHD Focus is built around how the ADHD brain works. It includes timed focus sessions with built-in breaks, gamified rewards to maintain motivation, streak tracking that does not punish missed days, and a distraction blocker. Tasks are organized by energy level and urgency, not just deadlines.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use it without a diagnosis?">
    Absolutely. You do not need a formal ADHD diagnosis to use ADHD Focus. The app is designed for anyone who wants better focus, habit building, and task management.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does it sync across devices?">
    Yes. Your data syncs automatically across all devices where you are signed in. Free accounts sync across two devices. Pro accounts sync across unlimited devices.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I reset my streak?">
    You cannot manually reset a streak, but a missed day breaks it naturally. If you want a fresh start, you can archive the old habit and create a new one. ADHD Focus also offers a "gentle streak" option in Pro that focuses on consistency over perfection.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my data if I cancel Pro?">
    Your data stays safe. When Pro ends, your account switches to the Free plan. You keep all your history, but you are limited to 3 active habits and 25-minute focus sessions. Nothing is deleted.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I share my progress with a coach or therapist?">
    Yes. Pro users can generate a weekly summary report from the **Profile** tab and share it via email or PDF. This includes focus time, habit consistency, and task completion rates.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a family or student discount?">
    Yes. We offer a 40% student discount with a valid student email, and a family plan that covers up to 5 profiles at a reduced rate. Contact support from the app to verify eligibility.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
