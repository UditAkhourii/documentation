If something is not working as expected, you are not alone. Most issues in ADHD Focus can be fixed in a few minutes with the steps below. Try the fix that matches your symptom, and reach out if you need more help.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Notifications not appearing">
    **Symptoms**: You do not get reminders for habits, focus session alerts, or daily prompts.

    **Steps to fix**:

    1. Open your device **Settings** \> **Notifications** \> **ADHD Focus** and make sure notifications are allowed.
    2. In the ADHD Focus app, go to **Settings** \> **Notifications** and confirm the toggles you want are turned on.
    3. If you use Do Not Disturb or Focus mode on your device, add ADHD Focus as an allowed app.
    4. Restart the app.

    **When to contact support**: If notifications still do not appear after checking device and app settings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="App not syncing across devices">
    **Symptoms**: Changes made on one device do not show up on another.

    **Steps to fix**:

    1. Make sure you are signed in to the same account on all devices.
    2. Pull down on the home screen to trigger a manual sync.
    3. Check that you have an active internet connection.
    4. Force-close and reopen the app.
    5. If you are on the Free plan, confirm you are not signed in on more than two devices.

    **When to contact support**: If sync fails after trying the steps above on a stable connection.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can't log in">
    **Symptoms**: You see an error when entering your email or password, or the login screen loops.

    **Steps to fix**:

    1. Double-check your email for typos.
    2. Tap **Forgot Password** and follow the reset link in your email.
    3. If you signed up with Apple, Google, or another provider, use that same button to log in.
    4. Clear the app cache or reinstall the app if the screen freezes.

    **When to contact support**: If you still cannot access your account after resetting your password.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Focus session timer not starting">
    **Symptoms**: You tap Start but the timer stays at zero or freezes.

    **Steps to fix**:

    1. Close any other apps that use audio or timers in the background.
    2. Make sure ADHD Focus has permission to run in the background in your device settings.
    3. Restart the app and try again.
    4. Check for an app update in your app store.

    **When to contact support**: If the timer fails on every session even after restarting the app.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Habit streak seems wrong">
    **Symptoms**: A streak shows more or fewer days than you expect.

    **Steps to fix**:

    1. Tap the habit to open its detail view and review the completion history.
    2. Check your timezone in **Settings** \> **Preferences**. A mismatched timezone can shift day boundaries.
    3. If you use the gentle streak feature, note that it counts consistent weeks rather than strict daily streaks.

    **When to contact support**: If the history clearly shows incorrect dates or missing completions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Lost reward points">
    **Symptoms**: Your reward points or unlocked items disappeared or dropped unexpectedly.

    **Steps to fix**:

    1. Go to **Rewards** and pull down to refresh.
    2. Check if you are signed in to the correct account.
    3. Review your recent activity feed for any redemptions or adjustments.
    4. Force-close and reopen the app to trigger a sync.

    **When to contact support**: If points are still missing after refreshing and confirming the correct account.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  Still stuck? Email us at [**support@adhdfs.com**](mailto:support@adhdfs.com) with your account email, a description of the issue, and any error messages you see. We typically respond within one business day.
</Note>
